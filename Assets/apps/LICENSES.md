Brand logos remain trademarks of their owners; used only to identify the source app.

## Extracted associated icons

`*.png` files may also be generated from locally installed apps via Windows
`ExtractAssociatedIcon` (the icon already on the user’s machine). These are
not redistributed as separate brand assets beyond what the installed app provides.
